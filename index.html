Common questions

Why can't I see other apps' processes on iPhone? iOS sandboxes every app, so no app can list or quit other processes. On iOS SysDeck shows the system-wide figures the OS does expose (overall CPU, memory, battery, thermal, storage, network). The full process list is a macOS feature.

Quitting a process says "not permitted". Some processes belong to other users or the system and can't be quit without elevated privileges. SysDeck can always quit processes you own.
