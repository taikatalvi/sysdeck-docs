FAQ

Good to know

Why can't I see other apps' processes on iPhone?

iOS sandboxes every app, so none can list or quit other processes. On iOS SysDeck shows the system-wide figures the OS exposes; the full process list is a macOS feature.

Quitting a process says "not permitted".

Some processes belong to other users or the system and need elevated privileges. Install the privileged helper (macOS) to read and manage all processes, or SysDeck will always let you quit ones you own.

Does SysDeck collect any data?

No. Metrics are read locally and never leave your device. There are no accounts and no analytics; purchases are handled by Apple.
