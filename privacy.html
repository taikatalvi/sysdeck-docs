Privacy Policy

SYSDECK · LAST UPDATED AUGUST 2026

SysDeck is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

Data we collect

None. SysDeck does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal data or usage analytics. There are no accounts and no tracking.

System metrics

SysDeck reads system statistics — such as CPU load, memory usage, disk and network activity, battery and thermal state, and (on macOS) the list of running processes — directly from the operating system. This information is displayed on your device only. It is never sent off the device, and any history you keep is stored locally.

Purchases

SysDeck Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the transaction and manages your subscription; SysDeck only receives from Apple whether Pro is active. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you enable alerts (a Pro feature), SysDeck posts local notifications on your device when a threshold you configured is crossed. These are generated and shown locally; nothing is sent to us.

Third parties

SysDeck uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
